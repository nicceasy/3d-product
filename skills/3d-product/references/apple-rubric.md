# The Apple rubric (the quality bar for packshots)

Measured on 18 apple.com newsroom hero images (reference only — never publish them). Grade 1–5 each; items 1, 2, 6
(fill) and 8 (neutrality) can be scored automatically from the image, the rest by eye.

1. **Background** — exactly #FAFAFC (2025–26 newsroom white) / #F5F5F7 / #000000; no visible floor or banding (±2 RGB).
2. **Highlights** — clipping (≥254) ≤ 0.5 % (refs median 0.1 %); on black, highlights (≥228) ≤ 3 % of the product:
   thin lines, never glows.
3. **Edges** — every silhouette reads through a light line or grey falloff; white-on-white stays separate without an
   outline.
4. **Reflections** — one smooth gradient per face; dark cards where the form needs them; no environment clutter.
5. **Camera** — vertical convergence ≤ 0.5°; height at the midline to 10° above; DOF only in macro.
6. **Composition** — centred hero filling 55–78 % of frame height, or a deliberate edge crop; generous negative space.
7. **Materials** — grainless bead-blast sheen, low-chroma anodising, knit cells resolved, frosted glass, chamfers
   catching light.
8. **Grounding & cleanliness** — floating or a tiny contact shade; no noise or fireflies; greys neutral within ±3 RGB.

Measured facts worth remembering: white products render *darker* than the ground (median 190–231 on 250); dark-shot
metal medians 45–80; the references themselves average ≈ 4.2 on the automated half (macros and x-rays break the
framing items by genre, so don't grade them on those). Photographer reference: Peter Belanger (view camera, each light
a separate exposure layer).

Shot grammar for launch films: dark open → light-sweep reveal → macro detail → states/ritual → exploded view →
material close-up → lifestyle → colour lineup (orbit) → name lockup.
